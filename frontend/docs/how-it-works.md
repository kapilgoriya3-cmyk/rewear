## Section 01 — The Flow

Eyebrow:

01 — THE FLOW

Heading:

Six steps. One complete swap.

Create six numbered cards in a timeline/grid layout.

### 01 — List your piece
Add photos, clothing type, size, brand, condition, location and an estimated swap value.

### 02 — Discover a match
Browse the community wardrobe and filter by category, size and location.

### 03 — Compare value
See estimated values for both items to make the proposed exchange easier to judge.

### 04 — Send a request
Choose the item you want to offer and send a swap request to the owner.

### 05 — Chat & negotiate
Discuss condition, photos, timing and exchange method before confirming.

### 06 — Exchange
Meet nearby or arrange a courier, then track the swap through completion.

## Section 04 — Before You Confirm

Eyebrow:

04 — BEFORE YOU CONFIRM

Heading:

Keep the exchange clear.

Create four clean information cards:

### Check the condition
Review photos and ask questions about wear, damage, sizing or anything else that matters before confirming.

### Agree on the handover
Confirm whether you are meeting nearby or arranging a courier before the exchange is finalized.

### Use the estimated value as guidance
The suggested ₹ value helps compare pieces, but both members decide whether the final swap feels fair.

### Track the status
Keep the exchange organized by tracking requests, confirmation and completion through ReWear.
